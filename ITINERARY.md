## Thursday 22 October: land, then one dinner near the hotel

Sleep at the Xujiahui hotel. There is no morning departure.

A published metro routing from the airport to Yishan Road and Zhongshan West Road is about 90 minutes: Metro Line 2, change at Century Avenue, then Metro Line 9 to Yishan Road. I do not have an official taxi-minute count for this hotel on 22 October. Plan on reaching the hotel sometime between mid-afternoon and early evening. The hotel text published on Trip.com says the walk from Yishan Road Station Exit 5 is about 300 meters and 4 minutes. Another field on the same page says 0.53 km. How to get there once you have slept a little: from Yishan Road, Metro Line 9 toward Century Avenue, three stops, Xujiahui, Zhaojiabang Road, Jiashan Road. Apple Maps puts the restaurant about 230 meters from Jiashan Road Exit 5. Come back the same way. If the call does not get a table, eat a seated meal in the Xujiahui malls at the Line 9 stop and keep Renheguan for another night this week.
