## Friday 23 October: Hangzhou, one canal district

Sleep at the Xujiahui hotel. Leave the hotel at 08:00.

Hongqiao Railway Station is the right Shanghai station for this day. It is the station with the frequent direct high-speed trains to Hangzhou East, and Hangzhou East is the end that connects to the canal by metro. Shanghai Railway Station is a simpler ride from Xujiahui, Line 9 one stop to Xujiahui and then Line 1, but it has fewer fast trains to Hangzhou East. Shanghai South is closer to the hotel, about 4 to 5 km on hotel listings, and it is the wrong end: those trains more often serve slower trips or Hangzhou South, which is across the river from the canal. Use Hongqiao for both day trips so the family learns one station.

Door to door from the hotel: walk to Yishan Road Station, about 4 minutes if the hotel text is right. Take Metro Line 3 or Line 4 one stop to Hongqiao Road (direction Jiangyang North Road on Line 3, or the Line 4 train whose next stop is Hongqiao Road). Change to Metro Line 10 toward Hongqiao Railway Station, eight stops. A third-party planner puts that ride at about 31 minutes and 4 yuan, with a transfer walk of about 260 meters. Add the hotel walk, the transfer, and time to reach the platform. Being at Hongqiao around 09:00 to 09:30 is a realistic reading of those figures. It is not a measured 23 October trip.

The exact 23 October train is in the booking, so it is not reprinted here. A timetable aggregator checked on 3 October 2026 shows direct G trains from Shanghai Hongqiao to Hangzhou East commonly listed at about 45 minutes, with a published full second-class fare often shown at 87 yuan. Those rows are the pattern, not a substitute for the booked departure. A morning train that reaches Hangzhou East late morning fits the museum hours.

In Hangzhou, the day stays in Gongshu, on the Grand Canal. From Hangzhou East, take Metro Line 1 toward Xianghu and change at Datieguan to Metro Line 5 toward Nanhudong. Get off at Gongchenqiao East. The museum's own note says to walk along Quzhou Street to the canal plaza. A 2024 local transport note says Exit B and about 500 meters. If the exit signs differ, follow the plaza. I do not have an official minute count for this metro ride, so I am not printing one.

The current reason to be here is the China Grand Canal Museum, 1 Canal Cultural Plaza, Gongshu. The standing hours on the museum site are Tuesday to Sunday, 09:00 to 16:30, last entry 16:00, closed Monday, free. Friday 23 October is an open day. On 25 September 2026 the local paper opened "运博20年 何以动人," a 20th-anniversary show in the second-floor temporary gallery, and said it runs through 31 December 2026. That show is the stop. Give it about 90 minutes, then sit down.

The one classic is Gongchen Bridge, which stands beside the museum. Look at it from the plaza. There is no need to turn it into a climb.

Lunch is a seated meal on Xiaohe Straight Street, the canal neighborhood north of the bridge, in the same district. I did not verify a single restaurant's hours for 23 October 2026, so choose a place with chairs by the water when you arrive. Eat before anyone is tired.

On the same waterside walk, the China Grand Canal Copper Culture Art Center opened under Qinjian Bridge in mid-August 2026. The Gongshu government notice describes it as a new copper-art space and does not publish hours. Step in if the door is open. If it is shut, the day does not change.

Aim to leave the canal by about 15:30 and take the booked Hangzhou East to Shanghai Hongqiao train in the late afternoon. Be back at the hotel for a quiet night.

Skip these, and why:

- West Lake, Lingyin, Longjing, Meijiawu, and Longwu. They are a second district, and Longwu is part of the packaged Jiangnan day the family already turned down.
- Wuzhen. The direct Hongqiao to Hangzhou East train does not drop you in the water town. Wuzhen sits off a different station, then needs another local ride. It is the centerpiece of the packaged tour, and adding it breaks this day.
- The Xiaohe Park "tank" market and Chiharu Shiota show. Published dates run through 11 October 2026. They are over before you arrive.
- The Tianmuli osmanthus fair. Published dates are 1 to 5 October 2026 only. I also did not find a verified osmanthus forecast for 23 October 2026, so this plan does not promise blossoms.
- The other National Day canal markets around Dadou Road and Canal Tiandi. The notices I found end on 7 October 2026.

## Sunday 25 October: Suzhou, the new museum on the lake

Sleep at the Xujiahui hotel. Leave at 08:15.

Go to Hongqiao again, by the same Line 3 or 4 then Line 10 route as Friday. The Suzhou stop that matches the day is Suzhou Industrial Park Station (苏州园区站), not Suzhou Station and not Suzhou North. Suzhou Museum of Contemporary Art's own visitor guide says: from Suzhou Industrial Park Station, Metro Line 8 direct to Suzhou MoCA Station, Exit 1. From Suzhou Station it is two lines, Line 4 to Sunwu Memorial Park and then Line 8. From Suzhou North it is also two lines. Hongqiao is the Shanghai end that current tables show running direct to Suzhou Industrial Park Station.

The booked train is the one to ride. An aggregator checked on 3 October 2026 lists direct Hongqiao to Suzhou Industrial Park trains at about 24 to 30 minutes, with second class often shown around 41 yuan on the faster G trains and lower on some D trains. That is the pattern, not a substitute for the ticket. A late-morning arrival puts you at the museum around 11:30 to 12:00. If the booking arrives at Suzhou Station instead, the museum's own backup is Line 4 and then Line 8.

The museum is at 99 You'an Street, Suzhou Industrial Park, on the right bank of Jinji Lake, next to the Suzhou Eye wheel. It opened to the public on 30 August 2026. Sunday hours are 10:00 to 17:00, last entry 16:00. Monday is closed. The opening season runs through 31 December 2026. On 25 October these shows are still inside their published dates:

- "Picasso and the World of His Influence," 30 August to 8 November 2026
- "Gen: A Journey Through Art's Transcendence," 30 August to 8 November 2026
- "Code and Canopy," 30 August to 15 November 2026

One ticket covers the shows. The industrial-park government said opening-season tickets start at 80 yuan, and a local report said weekday tickets are cheaper than weekend tickets. After lunch, the easy outdoor piece is the lake edge outside the same building. Sit. Look at the wheel. You do not need to ride it. I did not find a verified ticket price or Sunday operating notice for the wheel, so it stays optional.

The return is the booked train. A departure after 16:00 keeps the last entry from becoming a rush. If that ticket leaves from Suzhou Station, move there on Line 8 and Line 4. Be back at the hotel in the evening.
