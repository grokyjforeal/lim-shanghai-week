# Shanghai, 22 to 28 October 2026

The Lim family week, as a small site. Xujiahui is the hotel. Friday is Hangzhou in the rain, Hubin and in77. Sunday is Suzhou old town, the Humble Administrator's Garden and Pingjiang Road in the rain. Saturday is Chee Onn's birthday, Wukang Road and the river boat.

The research notes, with sources, are in [ITINERARY.md](ITINERARY.md). Confirmation numbers were not included, so the site does not invent them.
